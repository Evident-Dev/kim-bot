Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

KIM is a private Discord bot made and maintained by the Warframe LFG team. We keep the bare minimum KIM needs to run squads, and nothing else. We never sell, share or use your data for advertising, tracking or analytics.

What KIM does not collect

  • Messages. KIM can't read chat messages. It doesn't ask Discord for message content.
  • Voice. KIM never listens to or records voice.
  • Passwords. KIM never sees or stores a password. Sign-in uses Discord's own login.
  • Email addresses, real names, payment details or your Discord friends list.

A Discord user ID is a public number Discord gives every account. KIM's admin sign-in asks Discord only for basic profile info (the identify permission), not your email or servers.

Clans and server boosts

Creating a clan is a perk for people who boost the server. KIM checks with Discord whether each clan owner still boosts the server, about once an hour. KIM does not store this; it only stores the date it first saw an owner stop boosting. If the owner doesn't boost again within 14 days, KIM disbands the clan.

Where it's kept

KIM's data is kept in a private database run by the Warframe LFG team. It is not shared with anyone. Discord itself handles your account and messages under Discord's Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Don't want KIM to store anything about you? Don't use KIM hub channels.
  • To see or delete what KIM has stored about you, message a moderator in the Warframe LFG Discord server. We'll delete it within 30 days.

Children

KIM is for Discord users. Discord requires users to be at least 13 (or older where local law says so).
