KIM

A voice-squad bot for Warframe LFG on Discord.

KIM helps players find a squad. Join a hub voice channel and KIM makes a squad channel for you, names it after the activity you're running, and gives you a control panel to manage it. When everyone leaves, KIM deletes the channel.

Join to createJoin a hub, get your own squad channel.
Squad setupPick a fissure, relic, region or activity. KIM names the channel.
Owner controlsRename, limit, lock, hide, trust, block, kick, transfer.

A private project

KIM is a private, non-commercial project. It runs in one Discord server and is not available to add to other servers. There's no company behind it: it is made and maintained by the Warframe LFG team.
